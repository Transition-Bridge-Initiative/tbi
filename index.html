Transition Bridge Initiative

New Path. Stronger Future.

About Us

We support youth aged 17–22 in Calgary, Alberta who are living with physical, intellectual, or developmental disabilities, dual diagnoses, or co-occurring mental health and substance use challenges.

Our focus is on helping young people as they transition from child-focused services into adult systems of care. When youth age out of Family Support for Children with Disabilities (FSCD) at 18, they often experience a gap before receiving adult supports through Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD). This delay in services can lead to challenges in accessing housing, education, employment, and therapy. We aim to bridge this service gap by promoting continuity of care and reducing disruptions during this important transition. Our goal is to ensure youth remain supported, included, and connected to their communities as they move into adulthood.

Description of the Transition Bridge Initiative (TBI)

The Transition Bridge Initiative is an interim support program to address the most critical service gap between FSCD transitioning to the PDD service system. The program is designed to support youth ages 17-22 years old with developmental disabilities who are awaiting service adult funding through PDD.

This agency targets individuals who may lack permanent housing, family support, or formal medical documentation with no established guardianship. Particularly for those individuals experiencing living instability, under supported living facility, or out of home program funded by FSCD who are at risk of homelessness, crisis, ineligibility of funding, and loss of continuity care.

TBI will operate five days in a week following a community-based hybrid model merging in-center programming with community access, providing both development of skills and continuity of care.

Vision

To create a future where every youth with a disability achieves a successful transition to adulthood, equipped with the confidence to live a safe and independent life.

Mission

The Transition Bridge Initiative empowers youth with disabilities to move safely into adult services by providing housing, life skills, and hands-on support. Guided by respect and safety, our mission is to ensure no young person loses their support system as they build an independent life.

Programs/Services:

01

Transitional Housing / Supported living

this is short term safe housing (shared apartments, group homes, or emergency sheltered apartments) which includes ongoing supervision and support ensuring safety and stability. Daily routine supports involve meals, hygiene, budgeting, and laundry while having a caseworker or life skills mentor on site.

02

Life Skills Training

provides structured hands-on training sessions with living skills such as cooking, cleaning, shopping and budgeting, community navigation (transits), personal hygiene, social skills, and self-advocacy.

03

Health and Assessment Navigation

connect youth to medical assessments and subsidized services through AHS and community clinics to access medical professionals (psychologists, psychiatrists, developmental pediatricians, FASD and ASD clinics). This includes coordinating paperworks and consents for diagnostics while tracking functional limitations.

04

Mental Health

provision of trauma informed case managements for youths who are vulnerable with depression, behavioral crises, and social isolation, thus, access to counselling through community mental health is supported while waiting for formal diagnosis.

05

Employment readiness (prevocational training)

skill building in employment education such as pre-employment training (resume making, interview skills, and social skills), including life-skills integration or adult education program.

06

Case Management Navigation

assist individuals registering to government programs such as AISH, TAP, and Alberta Health. Help secure diagnostic assessments, coordinate to mental health support services, and keep records of functional progress for PDD eligibility and ongoing application.

07

Agency Matching

a program focusing on transition match hubs to navigate the adult disability system. This aspires to collaborate with existing adult long-term services such as SCOPE, Vecova, and DDRC aiming for a best fit disability support agency for an individual based on their diagnostics, comprehensive needs, and preferences assessment.
