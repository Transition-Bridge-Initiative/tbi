New Path. Stronger Future. Supporting youth through the most critical service gap into adult systems of care.

Bridging the Gap

We support youth aged 17–22 in Calgary, Alberta who are living with physical, intellectual, or developmental disabilities, dual diagnoses, or co-occurring mental health and substance use challenges.

When youth age out of Family Support for Children with Disabilities (FSCD) at 18, they often experience a severe gap before receiving adult supports through Persons with Developmental Disabilities (PDD). This delay can lead to critical challenges in housing, education, and therapy.

Our goal is to ensure youth remain supported, included, and connected to their communities as they move into adulthood.

Our Services

01

Transitional Housing

Short term safe housing which includes ongoing supervision and support ensuring safety and stability.

02

Life Skills Training

Structured hands-on training sessions with living skills such as cooking, cleaning, shopping and budgeting.

03

Health Navigation

Connect youth to medical assessments and subsidized services through AHS and community clinics.

04

Mental Health

Provision of trauma informed case managements for youths vulnerable to depression or social isolation.

05

Employment Readiness

Skill building in employment education such as pre-employment training, resume making, and interviews.

06

Agency Matching

A program focusing on transition match hubs to navigate the adult disability system for a best fit support agency.
